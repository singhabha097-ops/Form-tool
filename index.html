Privacy Policy – Form Tools

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Form Tools ("the app", "we") is a free utility app for QR scanning and generation, photo resizing, image compression, image-to-PDF conversion, passport photo and signature resizing. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

1. Your photos and files stay on your phone

All image, PDF and QR processing happens on your device. We do not upload, store or have access to your photos, signatures, PDFs, QR codes or scan results. The app works offline.

2. Permissions we use

  • Camera: only to scan QR codes. Camera images are processed on your device and are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Storage / media: only to save files you create to your Pictures or Downloads folder. On Android 9 and below, the app asks for storage permission for this. Photos you choose are read through the system picker.
  • Internet: used only to show ads and, in future, to process purchases.

3. Information stored on your device

The app stores your QR scan history, your Premium status and a daily save counter locally on your phone. You can delete scan history inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

4. Advertising

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect and use certain information, such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. We do not receive your personal information from this process. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Learn more: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Premium users do not see ads.

5. Purchases

Premium purchases, if offered, are processed by the app store you downloaded the app from (for example Samsung Galaxy Store). We do not see or store your payment details.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above. Files are shared only when you choose to share them using your phone's share menu.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

Because your files are processed and stored on your own device, their security depends on your device's protection. We recommend keeping your phone locked and updated.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above updated.
